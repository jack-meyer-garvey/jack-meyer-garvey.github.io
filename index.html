A Comparison of Schemes for Age-Structured Population Models Current - Started Spring 2026

A numerical study of various schemes for solving multi-compartment age-structured population models, joint with Dr. Riujin Zhao (Minnesota State University).

Verifying Coppersmith’s Attack in Isabelle/HOL Current - Started Fall 2024

A machine-verified formalization of Coppersmith’s lattice-based attack on RSA, joint with Dr. Katherine Kosaian (University of Iowa). The proof file establishes that the method recovers small roots up to the known optimal bound. We have already verified the correctness. We are currently working on the time complexity analysis, which is the final piece needed to complete the paper.

A Hybrid Algorithm for the Word Problem on Semi-Thue Systems Presented in Spring 2024

A neural-guided heuristic for hard instances of the word problem in string-rewriting systems. The search alternates a double-ended breadth-first search with two guided random walks, each dynamically weighted toward the structural regularities observed at the opposite frontier.
